# Warehouse Management
## 📋 Overview
**Location:** `apps/warehouse_management/` | **Status:** ✅ Complete | **Completion:** 80%
## 🎯 Purpose
Central inventory and distribution with stock transfers, receiving, putaway, and fulfillment operations.
## ✅ What is Done
- Inventory dashboard (stock levels by location with status tracking)
- Stock transfers (inter-warehouse movements with shipment tracking)
- Receiving & putaway (inbound processing with discrepancy management)
- Distribution & fulfillment (outbound orders with pick/pack workflows)
- Amber theme
