# Reports & Analytics
## 📋 Overview
**Location:** `apps/reports_analytics/` | **Status:** ✅ Complete | **Completion:** 80%
## 🎯 Purpose
Business intelligence dashboards with sales, performance, inventory, and staff analytics for data-driven decisions.
## ✅ What is Done
- Sales reports (daily, weekly, monthly, yearly) with charts
- Performance metrics (revenue, orders, avg ticket)
- Inventory reports (stock levels, movements, valuations)
- Staff analytics (performance, hours, productivity)
- Custom report builder with filters and date ranges
- fl_chart visualizations (bar, line, pie charts)
- Blue theme, Material 3
